The Standard

Five categories. No exceptions.

01 / PHONE

No social media, short-form scrolling, or recreational use around wake/sleep.

02 / BODY

Scheduled training, daily outdoor movement, cold shower, real sleep.

03 / FUEL

Whole foods only. No processed foods. Water or milk — nothing else.

04 / MIND

Mission work, reading, and facing the one task you're avoiding.

05 / INTEGRITY

No lying about progress. No faked proof. That's an automatic out.

Verification

You don't grade yourself.

Every day you log proof — meals, workouts, mission work, screen time. You don't submit all of it. One person gets randomly pulled for audit each day, and it can be you again tomorrow.
Challenge Days

Final Crucible
Full weighted Murph. Plus mission work, reading, and the full daily standard. If it's too close to call, survivors can unanimously vote to extend once, to Day 35.
35
Overtime Final
Only happens if the group chooses to extend past Day 30. Same test, no shortcuts: the full weighted Murph again. No eliminated participant re-enters, and the result is final.
